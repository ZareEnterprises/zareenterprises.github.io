The Boston Foundation

PA'LANTE 2026

📅 Date: 09/2026 📍 Venue: Termeer Square, Boston 👥 400 Assistants 🎵 Style: Latin Music

What They Needed

For PA'LANTE 2025, The Boston Foundation required a production partner capable of delivering a seamless, high-quality live experience while coordinating every technical and operational element behind the scenes. The event brought together community leaders, philanthropists, elected officials, corporate partners, and hundreds of guests, making flawless execution essential.

Beyond producing the headline performance, the Foundation needed comprehensive planning and coordination across staging, audio, lighting, show flow, artist logistics, and vendor management to ensure every element aligned with the event's fundraising mission. The objective was to create a polished and engaging production that celebrated Latino culture, elevated the guest experience, and supported an evening designed to inspire generosity and strengthen community connections.

What AMBRA Delivers

Musicians

Barrio Latino, a 15-piece professional Latin orchestra featuring musicians from Boston's leading music institutions, with 5 extra guests for specific songs.

Duration

A fully curated 2h30 minute headline performance, designed exclusively for Pa'Lante 2025. The band stays from 3pm to 11pm.

Music Customization

Original medleys and custom arrangements, including tributes to Willie Colón, Mexican music, Brazilian music, and a contemporary Venezuelan tribute.

Repertoire

A diverse musical journey spanning salsa, merengue, bachata, Latin pop, ballads, jazz influences, and modern Latin hits — representing Mexico, Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Cuba, Venezuela, Colombia, Brazil and Peru.

Planning

Seven-month production planning, beginning in February 2026, with monthly strategic meetings to develop the show's artistic vision, timeline, and execution plan.

Vendor & Rentals

Comprehensive production coordination, including stage, sound, and lighting documentation, run-of-show planning, technical logistics, and the overall creative concept.

Talent Acquisition

Acquisition and coordination for more than 40 musicians, vocalists, dancers, arrangers, engineers, and production staff.

On-Site Direction

On-site production leadership, serving as the central point of coordination between performers, technical crews, event planners, and venue staff.
