✺ Frequently asked questions ✺

Where are you based, and do you perform outside of Boston?

We are based in Boston but happily travel for events. Please note that mobility fees are calculated separately from the total package price.

How do you determine the number of musicians for an event?

We collaborate closely with you to choose the ideal number of musicians based on your event's size, ambiance, and musical needs, ensuring a perfect match.

What styles of music do you perform?

Our repertoire is highly versatile, with a specialization in Latin music. From salsa, cumbia, bachata, boleros, ballads, and bossa nova to jazz, funk, and pop, we curate each performance to reflect the unique atmosphere and vision of your event.

How far in advance should we book your services?

For the best availability, we recommend booking as soon as possible, especially for popular dates. This allows us to fully plan and tailor our performance to your event.

Do you provide sound equipment?

Yes, for small concerts we use top-tier, professional-grade sound equipment. And for big concerts (more than 8 musicians) we contact vendors to give you a 360 budget covering all expenses.

What is included in the pricing?

Our pricing covers the performance, sound equipment, transportation and event tailored planning support.

Do you work with a DJ?

Absolutely! We offer the flexibility of booking a solo DJ or integrating a DJ into your live set.
