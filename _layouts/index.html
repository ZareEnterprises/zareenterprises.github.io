How It Works

From first call to final bow

01

Discovery & Planning (AMBRA App)

We define what the event needs to achieve and build the production plan around it. From day one, you follow every update and decision in real time through the AMBRA app.

02

Venue Mapping & Curation

We recreate your venue in 3D to plan every placement and cue, then hand-pick musicians from our conservatory network and build the setlist with you.

03

Production Planning

Stage, dancers, DJ, sound and lighting, all contracted and coordinated by us well in advance. Every song is transcribed from scratch and arranged for your event.

04

Rehearsals

Attire, stage movement and four full rehearsals for large events. Every set is played, tested and refined before your guests hear a single note.

05

Show Day

Our crew builds the stage and runs the audio while the whole team works from one live run-of-show in the AMBRA app, so every cue lands on time.
