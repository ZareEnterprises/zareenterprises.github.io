Service Terms & Conditions

This Service Agreement is entered into on the invoice issue date between (“AMBRA”), a limited liability company organized under the laws of the United States, and the client named on page 1 (the “Client”). It sets the terms under which AMBRA provides the entertainment and production services described in this invoice.

01
Scope of services
AMBRA will provide the services described in this invoice.
02
Event schedule
Services take place on the date shown on page 1. Setup, performance and breakdown follow the agreed schedule. AMBRA is not obligated to extend performance time or move the agreed time slot unless both parties approve it in writing beforehand. Additional time requested during the event may incur extra fees, subject to performer availability.
03
Payment
A deposit of of the total is required, and services are confirmed only once it is received. The remaining balance is due no later than days before the event, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Payments are processed through Stripe via the payment links provided. AMBRA may withhold services if full payment has not been received by that deadline.
04
Cancellation & refunds
  • Client cancels 14 days or more before the event: 50% of the total paid is refunded. AMBRA retains the rest to cover preparation, reserved personnel and administrative costs.
  • Client cancels 3 days or less before the event: no refund.
  • AMBRA cancels for reasons within its control: full refund of all payments made.
  • Force majeure (natural disasters, extreme weather, government restrictions, venue closures, public emergencies or other circumstances beyond either party’s control): neither party is liable for non-performance, and the full amount is refunded.
05
Event conditions
The Client is responsible for:
  • Adequate space and safe conditions for performers and equipment.
  • Venue access and electricity at the agreed setup time.
  • Compliance with local noise regulations, venue rules and any required event permits.
  • A clean, unobstructed performance area suitable for the contracted services.
  • An adequate sound system that meets the agreed requirements.
06
Damages
The Client is responsible for any damage to equipment caused by guests, staff or venue conditions during the event. Repair or replacement costs may be billed to the Client.
07
Acceptance
Payment of this invoice or written confirmation of the booking constitutes acceptance of these terms. By paying the requested amount, the Client confirms they have read, understood and agree to be bound by them.
